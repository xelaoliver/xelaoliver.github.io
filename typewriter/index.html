Typewritist

February, 2026

Next to my desktop there are 2 mechanical typewriters vertically stacked on shelves and an electric one bunged into my wardrobe.

I find them so mechanically fascinating. The simple gesture of pressing a key, actuating a type lever and striking a type bar against some ink ribbon to analoguely print a character is an artform. Back when everyone had a typewriter and computer from their parents, people would scan in the printed typeface of their typewriter and photoshop their own digital-analogue texts. I was inspired by this, so I did it with my favourite typewriter: a 1960s Adler Tippa Typewriter, all yellowed and old.
